# Economic Universe
## Economic explanation for each pair being traded
Viable long-run pairs were selected base on the following:
1. **Revenue beta**: the same commodity, product category, customer cohort, or macro demand driver.
2. **Cost beta**: similar exposure to feedstock, fright, labor, packaging, energy, adversiting, or financing costs. 
3. **Business-model symmetry**: upstream with upstream, refiners with refiners, etc...
4. **Geographic and currency symmetry**: similar tax, regulation, FX, and benchmark-price exposure. 
5. **Capital-cycle symmetry**: similar maintenance capital, reserve replacement, store expansion, or brand-investment requirements.
6. **Structural stability**: no pending merger, breakup, portfolio sale, or strategy pivot that changes the hedge ratio.
